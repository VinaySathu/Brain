        Thinking cost

        BRAIN uses the lowest-cost Claude tier for reading documents and checking for conflicts. Answers can use the same quick tier or the balanced tier.

        Answer model
        • Documents are hashed and never re-read when uploaded again.
        • Each document is analysed in chunks of about 9,000 characters, at most 30 chunks; text beyond that is still stored and searchable.
        • Questions retrieve the best-matching memories and passages locally first; only those are sent to Claude.
        • Conflict checks run only when a similar existing memory is found.

        Privacy

        • Your knowledge is stored in your private space in this artifact's database. Other people who open this artifact cannot read it, including the artifact's owner.
        • Document content is sent to Claude only to analyse it or answer your questions, on your own Claude account.
        • Text inside documents is treated as data. Instructions written inside a document are never followed.
        • Deleting a document permanently removes its text and every memory, concept and relationship derived from it.
